Our Story
Theresa. Owner. Usually chatting with customers.

One roof for the whole barnyard.

Animal Health Center started with a simple idea and evolved into more than that. The folks of SW Minnesota should not need to make multiple stops to have access to what they need for their pets and livestock. So we put it all under one roof. A full retail pet and livestock store, a pet resort for boarding and daycare, and on staff grooming.

I started here years before I ever owned the place. Back in 2000, to be exact. In 2020, I purchased the store I already loved from Dr. Mike Healy, and not much changed because it didn't need to. We added the boarding kennel to fill a need in the community, but we still kept it local, friendly, and ready for animals big and small.

We still know your dog by name. We remember the heifer you had last year that won the county fair. And we will always support you, your families, and the Marshall community that we love to be a part of.

Theresa

Owner, Animal Health Center and Pet Resort
